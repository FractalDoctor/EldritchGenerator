ELDRITCH GENERATOR

"That is not dead which can eternal lie..."

A procedural cosmic horror content generator for writers, Game Masters, and enthusiasts of the weird. Six generators, millions of unique combinations, no two results alike.

Generators

  • ENTITIES — cosmic beings with unpronounceable names, impossible forms, and unique procedural sigils
  • ARTEFACTS — cursed objects with provenance, supernatural properties, and the price they exact
  • TOMES — forbidden texts in impossible languages, with contents that damage the reader
  • CULTS — secret organisations with goals, methods, hierarchies, and recognition signals
  • PLACES — cursed locations where reality runs thin and the locals know to stay away
  • HOOKS — complete scenario seeds with setups, complications, stakes, and twists

Features

  • Tap any field to re-roll it independently
  • Save favourites with personal notes
  • Share formatted results via Messages, email, or any app
  • Browse your last three generations per tab
  • Adjust name complexity, description length, and tone
  • Four tone modes: Cosmic, Gothic, Clinical, Mythic
  • Works completely offline — no internet required
  • No AI, no tracking, no data collection

Every result is assembled from layered combinatorial tables — not pre-written entries, not AI. The generation pools contain thousands of entries producing trillions of unique combinations.
