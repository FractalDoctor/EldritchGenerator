ELDRITCH GENERATOR — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: March 2026

Data Collection

Eldritch Generator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind.

Network Requests

Eldritch Generator does not make any network requests. The app functions entirely offline with no server communication of any kind. All content is generated locally on your device from compiled data tables.

Local Data Storage

Eldritch Generator stores your saved favourites and generation settings locally on your device using SwiftData and UserDefaults respectively. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Analytics and Tracking

Eldritch Generator does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Third-Party Services

Eldritch Generator does not communicate with any third-party services.

Children's Privacy

Eldritch Generator does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is rated 12+ for infrequent mild horror and fear themes.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the Eldritch Generator GitHub repository.
